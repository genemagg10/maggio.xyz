a little note from

the baker

I'm Michelle, and I've been baking gluten-free and vegan for over 20 years. I got my start at Le Cordon Bleu pastry school, cooked for José Andrés at The Bazaar in Beverly Hills, then opened a gluten-free and vegan donut shop in the heart of the Los Angeles food scene. I bring all of that experience — and a lot of love — home to my family, who have celiac, keeping only the recipes everyone can't stop asking for. Everything that leaves the case is celiac-safe, tested on my own family first, and made to taste like nobody made a single compromise.

— Michelle
